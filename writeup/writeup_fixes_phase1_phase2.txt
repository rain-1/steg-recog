Suggested text:
  We use Qwen3-1.7B both to generate the watermarked text and as the model we train. The watermark
  splits the vocabulary with a random hyperplane in the model's token-embedding space: tokens on
  one side are purple, on the other orange. The cut is placed so ordinary text is 50% purple and
  50% orange by token frequency. Tokens without a Latin letter (punctuation), with digits or
  symbols, and special tokens are left neutral; about 86% of ordinary text is coloured. A text
  therefore carries one bit: purple or orange.

6. TODO "+5 sampling pressure, what?": before sampling each token, the generator adds 5 to the

Suggested text:

  far more words of one colour. We keep rewrites with a clear watermark (|z| >= 1.5, median 4.3)
  that pass a health check. The recognition set has 768 texts from 320 requests, half purple and

PASSIVE EXPOSURE: GIVE IT ITS OWN SECTION
-----------------------------------------
7. As a result section, state:
